# Source attribution

The CPU designs and experiment records were developed by Leo Itoh at The
University of Tokyo.
Original source bytes and any embedded notices are preserved in the exported
RTL snapshots.

CoreMark is an EEMBC benchmark. The CoreMark port used in the experiment prints an integer-only
score, which differs from the precise score
calculated from the measured tick count. CoreMark source and compiled firmware
are not included in this repository.

Conversation text retains its historical wording. Local filesystem prefixes
are replaced as described in the README.
